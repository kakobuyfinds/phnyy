The quick answer

What makes a spreadsheet useful?

A ZentBuy spreadsheet is a product-discovery list. Its value comes from clear source links, relevant item details and enough information to compare alternatives. Use this guide to assess those details, then explore the separate Hipobuyfind directory through the links above.

01 / A more useful shortlist

Three decisions before the cart.

Use ZentBuy finds as a starting point for research. A saved row should tell you why the item suits your needs.

01 — PURPOSE

Decide what it needs to do.

Everyday walking, layering and occasional wear create different requirements. Set your preferred fit, material and practical use before comparing silhouettes.

02 — EVIDENCE

Read beyond the cover photo.

Inspect the selected variant, size chart, source listing and return conditions. Save the original product identifier so a changed title does not erase your reference.

03 — TOTAL

Leave room for the whole cost.

Separate the item amount from delivery and other charges. Compare a current quote for your destination rather than treating a spreadsheet figure as a final total.

02 / Learn to read the details

A familiar logo is only one detail.

Whether you are comparing Nike sneakers, Louis Vuitton accessories, Gucci headwear, Hermès scarves or Dior footwear, brand recognition cannot replace measurements, clear photographs and provenance.

  1. 01
    Match the actual variant.Check model name, color, material description and selected size against the photos.
  2. 02
    Ask for useful views.For footwear, inspect both shoes, soles and labels. For clothing and accessories, check seams, closures and dimensions.
  3. 03
    Keep authenticity separate.QC photographs show visible condition. They do not establish authenticity or replace reliable provenance.
  4. 04
    Resolve missing information.If a seller cannot clarify fit, composition or return terms, leave the item off your shortlist until you have enough information.

04 / The reading room

Small guides. Better questions.

Read these short articles before narrowing your clothing, footwear and accessory choices.

Fit notes / Footwear

Sneaker sizing: begin with a pair you own

Measure the removable insole of a comfortable pair, then compare it with the seller’s model-specific chart. Check whether the chart describes foot length, insole length or an outer-sole measurement.

Ask for the missing measurement before choosing a size. A familiar size label alone cannot establish the fit of a different model.

  • Use the same unit for both measurements.
  • Consider width as well as length.
  • Confirm the selected size on the order.

ZentBuy sizing guide / Clothing

Clothing fit: compare the garment, not the letter

Lay a well-fitting garment flat. Record chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length and body length using the same method as the listing. Compare these measurements with the selected variant.

For matching sets, check the top and bottom separately. Ask about fabric composition and care instructions when they are absent.

  • Allow for your preferred layering.
  • Check whether waist measurements are relaxed or stretched.
  • Review return restrictions for intimate apparel.

Parcel planning / Costs

Shipping: separate the item from the parcel

Keep a separate estimate for item cost, domestic transport, service or payment fees, international delivery and destination charges. Ask which costs are included in the current quote.

Packaging may change the chargeable weight. Consolidation can change the total, but it does not automatically make every order cheaper.

  • Confirm the destination and available route.
  • Check how parcel weight is calculated.
  • Read the carrier and agent’s current conditions.

06 / Direct answers

Before you follow a link.

Understand what this guide provides and what you still need to confirm with the destination site or seller.

What is a ZentBuy spreadsheet?

A ZentBuy spreadsheet is a product-discovery list organized for people researching purchases through ZentBuy. A useful entry records the source link, item details, size information and notes needed to compare listings. This page is an independent reading guide, not a live spreadsheet or checkout.

Where do the browse buttons take me?

Category buttons open the corresponding category on hipobuyfind.com. All other navigation links open the Hipobuyfind homepage. They do not open a ZentBuy account, an individual product or a downloadable spreadsheet.

Can QC photos prove that an item is authentic?

No. QC photos can help you inspect visible details such as size labels, construction and apparent damage. They do not by themselves prove authenticity, material composition or long-term durability.

How should I choose a clothing or sneaker size?

Compare the listing’s model-specific measurements with a garment or pair you already wear comfortably. Check how the measurements were taken, and ask for clarification when a chart is incomplete. Do not rely only on a familiar size label.

Does a listed item price include international shipping?

Do not assume it does. Confirm the item price, domestic shipping, service or payment fees, international delivery and any destination charges separately before paying.

Is this page affiliated with ZentBuy or the pictured brands?

No official relationship is claimed. Brand names and reference images identify the styles discussed. They do not certify destination listings, establish product availability or imply endorsement.
